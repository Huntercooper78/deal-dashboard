Sale days

Add or refresh a sale day
  1. Open the sale day yourself on the auction site. Use the auction site and pick a date from its calendar.
  2. Click your Grab sale page bookmark (set up once, below). A small box appears on the page. Click Copy in it.
  3. Paste here and press Add to dashboard.

One-time bookmark setup: press Copy bookmark code, make a new bookmark of any page in your browser, edit its address, and paste this code in as the address. Name it Grab sale page. (Safari: Bookmarks, Add Bookmark, then Edit Address.) You can also try dragging Grab sale page to your bookmarks bar.

Move or back up your data: Copy my data gives you text with your sale days, notes and inputs. Paste it into the box and press Load my data on another device.

This reads the page you already have open. It does not visit the auction site by itself. Keep the listings for your own use.
